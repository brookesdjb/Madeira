## Branch policy

- Use `integration/ipad` for combined local builds and baseline/candidate tests.
- Keep each topic branch rooted in its intended upstream dependency, never in the
  combined integration history. Use merge commits in integration rather than
  repeatedly squashing topic changes, so ancestry stays traceable.
- Retain merged topic branches while they are candidates for upstream PRs. Closed
  fork review PRs do not prevent opening separate upstream PRs from those branches.
- Keep the older control checkout labelled stale: it is not a matched baseline
  for the current capture/replay/socket instrumentation. Establish fresh baselines
  after the socket transport is installed and physically verified.
- Old worktrees may contain ignored native archives, signed apps or private run
  evidence. No deletion was attempted; pruning needs an inventory of those files
  and confirmation of the exact disposable targets.
